Loading Wordmark — Animation Concepts

Two approaches for the "MOONLIT" loading splash. Dark background (#0D0D0D) matches the app.

M O O N L I T

A — Shimmer Sweep

A glow sweeps across the letters left-to-right, like a spotlight passing over each character. The word is always visible — the shimmer just moves through.

M O O N L I T

B — Media Logo Fade

Entire wordmark fades in, holds, then fades out — like the Netflix/HBO/Disney logo cards. Clean and cinematic. Loops with a subtle scale+blur dissolve.

Combined — Shimmer + Fade Hybrid

A third option: the fade-in/out cycle, but with the shimmer sweep during each "hold" phase.

M O O N L I T

C — Hybrid

The wordmark fades in (cinematic dissolve), then the shimmer sweeps across during the visible phase, then fades out. Best of both worlds — recognizable and active.
